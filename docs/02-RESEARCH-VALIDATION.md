## 2. Questions to validate the scenario

| Area | Questions | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| Shop and products | One shop or several? Which goods are sold? Are quantities whole numbers? Are goods weighed or packs opened into pieces? | Single shop, packaged goods, and no conversion are design assumptions. |
| Sales | Who records a sale? What is entered, calculated, and given to the customer? How is a past sale found? What happens when an entry is wrong? | Notebook sales and paper/manual retrieval are hypothetical. |
| Payments and receipts | Cash only or other methods? Any customer credit? Which currency, prices/taxes, and receipt fields apply? | Cash only is proposed. Currency, pricing/tax treatment, and receipt fields are unconfirmed. |
| Receiving | Who checks a delivery? Which reference, quantity, and cost fields are recorded? Are supplier balances essential? | Paper delivery records are hypothetical; supplier balances are excluded from the draft scope. |
| Stock counts | How is available stock known? When is it counted? Who records opening quantities, losses, damage, and count differences? | Shelf counts are hypothetical; opening-stock and correction adjustments are proposed. |
| Returns | Are returns accepted? Must they link to a receipt? Who approves them? Is cash refunded? How are saleable and damaged goods handled? | Sale-linked owner-processed refunds are proposed; actual policy is unconfirmed. |
| Corrections | How are errors in completed sales, deliveries, returns, and adjustments discovered and corrected? What records must be retained? | Posted-transaction correction/reversal policy is unresolved. |
| Users | Who sets prices, receives goods, sells, approves returns, manages accounts, and sees costs/reports? | Owner/manager and cashier are proposed roles. |
| Information needs | Which summaries are actually used? What date ranges, filters, printouts, low-stock thresholds, and sales visibility are needed? | Dashboard and report list are proposed. |
